The Pullman

Downtown Shakopee's
low-key living room

Pullman Club sits on 1st Ave W in the middle of downtown Shakopee — the kind of neighborhood bar where the pool table's always got next and the popcorn machine never stops. No pretense, no dress code, just cold drinks and regulars who feel like family.

Open Monday through Saturday, 8 in the morning to 1 at night, it's as good for an early beer as it is for last call. Darts, a TouchTunes jukebox, and contactless payment round out a spot built for showing up as you are.
